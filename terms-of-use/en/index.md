## 5. Information Accuracy and Functional Limitations

### Calendars and Date Conversion

Displayed dates are based on the App's calculation methods. Dates may differ from other calendars or official information because of calculation methods, regions, time zones, religious practices, astronomical observations, or decisions by religious organizations. The accuracy and completeness of calculated results, and agreement with all historical calendars, cannot be guaranteed. Check official information from the relevant authorities or organizations for public procedures, deadlines, religious events, and other important uses.

The App uses midnight in Japan for Kyureki, midnight in Jerusalem for the Hebrew calendar, and midnight in India for the Hindu lunisolar calendar as the basis for date display. In the App, Hebrew calendar dates change at midnight in Jerusalem rather than at sunset. The App's Hindu lunisolar calendar display uses the Amanta system, with day numbers based on elapsed days from the start of the month; it does not implement a religious calendar based on sunrise or tithi. Ethiopian date display uses 6 a.m. in Addis Ababa, with a different basis used for timed event entry. See [Calendar Help](../../helps/en/02-calendars.md#calendar-limits) for details of date boundaries and calculation methods. Use Rokuyo and solar terms as reference information as well.
