# Chapter 3 Viewing the Calendar

The main screen displays the selected calendar system in month view or list view. Select a date to view its holidays and events.

<a id="03-section-1"></a>

## Switch views

### Month view and list view

Tap <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/view-list.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="List"> (switch to list view) or <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/grid-view.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Four squares"> (switch to month view) at the bottom left of the screen. The list symbol appears in month view, and the four-square symbol appears in list view. The grid displays one month at a time. List view displays five consecutive months starting from the current month. Ads may appear on screen.

### Switch calendar systems

Tap the calendar system name and “▼” at the top left of the screen to choose an enabled calendar system. To enable calendar systems or change their order, use [calendar display settings](02-calendars.md#calendar-switch).

<a id="03-section-2"></a>

## Navigate to a date

### Move to the previous or next month

In month view, swipe left or right to move between months. In list view, scroll vertically to view five consecutive months starting from the current month. To move beyond this range, select a month using the year and month at the top of the screen. When changing dates or calendar systems, you may need to wait briefly for data to finish loading.

### Choose a year and month

Tap the year, month, and “▼” at the top center of the screen to open the year/month picker. Use the previous-year and next-year controls (“<” and “>”) to change years, then tap a month in the month list. You can swipe the month list left or right. Tap “×” at the top left of the picker to close it. You can navigate up to 200 years before or after the year shown when the picker was opened.

### Return to today

Tap Today to return to the current date. The app moves to the date corresponding to today in the displayed calendar system.

<a id="03-section-3"></a>

## View date and event details

### Select a date

Tap a date on the calendar to open its day details, showing holidays and events for that day. If there are no events, a message indicates this. Use your device's back action to close day details and return to the calendar.

Switching calendar systems does not automatically add every country's holidays. To display Japanese holidays, add the Japanese holiday preset from iCal subscriptions under <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/settings.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Gear"> (Settings) > event management. See [Adding a Public Calendar](08-subscriptions.md#ical-add) for the steps. The Japanese lunisolar calendar also displays the 24 solar terms.

### Select an event

Tap an event in day details or list view to open its details. Editable events show <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/edit.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Pencil"> (Edit) and <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/delete.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Trash can"> (Delete). These actions do not appear for read-only events, such as those from iCal subscriptions.

<a id="03-section-4"></a>

## Search and settings

Use <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/search.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Magnifying glass"> (Search) at the bottom of the main screen to search for events. Use <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/settings.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Gear"> (Settings) to open calendar system settings, event management, and theme settings. For search instructions, see [Search and Categories](06-search-categories.md). For display settings, see [Calendar Systems and Settings](02-calendars.md).
