# Chapter 9 Appearance and Widgets

You can set the app theme to follow your system settings or choose light or dark mode. You can also add a home screen widget that shows the Gregorian calendar alongside additional calendars.

<a id="09-section-1"></a>

## Change the theme

### Choose a theme mode

1. Tap <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/settings.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Gear"> (Settings) at the bottom of the main screen.
2. Open the theme settings.
3. Choose the system default, light mode, or dark mode.

Your choice takes effect immediately and is saved automatically. The system default follows your device's appearance settings.

<a id="widget-use"></a>

## Use widgets

### Add a widget to the home screen

Touch and hold your Android home screen, then select WorldCalendar from the widget list and place the widget. If a configuration screen opens while adding it, use the “☑ / ☐” checkboxes to select one or two additional calendars, then tap Save. If two calendars are already selected, clear an unwanted calendar before selecting another. The steps and labels vary depending on your home screen app.

The widget shows today's date in a separate column for each calendar. In addition to the Gregorian column, you select one or two calendars in the settings. The available choices are calendars other than Gregorian: Japanese lunisolar, Julian, Hebrew, Hindu, and Ethiopian. The selected columns follow the order in the calendar display settings.

### Change the calendars after adding a widget

Tap <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/widget-settings.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Gear"> (Settings) in the upper-right corner of the widget, select the additional calendars again, and tap Save. Tapping the body of the widget opens WorldCalendar. The widget updates when the date changes, the device time or time zone changes, and at other relevant times. Device or home screen app restrictions may delay updates.
