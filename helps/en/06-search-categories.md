# Chapter 6 Search and Organize Events

Search finds events stored in the app, connected device calendar events, and subscribed iCal events together. Categories help you organize local events.

<a id="search-events"></a>

## Search for events

### How to search

Tap <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/search.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Magnifying glass"> (Search) at the bottom of the main screen and enter a term in the search field. Results update shortly after you stop typing. Search matches are case-insensitive and can match part of the text. No results are shown when the search field is empty. Tap a result to open the event details. Use “×” on the right of the input field to clear the search term, or “←” at the top left to return to the calendar.

### Search content and date range

Search covers events within two years before and after the current date and time. It searches titles, descriptions, category names, and dates in the calendar currently displayed. Locations and source names are not searched. Events from disabled device calendars and subscribed calendars are excluded from the results.

Results are sorted by occurrence date and time, newest first. Recurring events are searched as occurrences within the search period. From the search screen, you can open event details and edit or delete events that support those actions.

<a id="category-manage"></a>

## Create and manage categories

### What categories do and their limits

You can assign categories to local events to organize them. You can create up to 50 categories, and at least one must remain.

### Add, edit, or delete a category

To add a category, open <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/settings.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Gear"> (Settings) > event management and tap “＋” to the right of the local section heading. Enter a category name, choose a color, and save. To edit a category, tap <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/edit.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Pencil"> (Edit) on its row, make your changes, and save. To delete one, use <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/delete.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Trash can"> (Delete) on its row. Deletion takes effect immediately, with no confirmation screen. The last remaining category cannot be deleted.

### Assign a category to an event

On the event creation or editing screen, choose local storage as the save destination, then select a category. You can select from the categories you have created. In-app categories cannot be selected for events stored elsewhere, such as in a device calendar. Use “☑／☐” (the checkbox) on each category row to toggle whether its events appear in the event list.

### What happens when you delete a category

Deleting a category removes its association with events that used it. The events themselves are not deleted. Turning off a category's visibility does not exclude its events from search. After deleting a category, you can no longer find those events by that category name.
