# Chapter 1 Getting Started

WorldCalendar is a calendar app that lets you view dates using several calendar systems, including the Gregorian calendar. Events and settings are generally stored on your device. You can also display events from device calendars and iCal calendars by connecting them.

This help identifies buttons without text by their symbol and function, such as “+” (add event). For buttons that are difficult to describe in text, it includes an icon showing their actual shape.

<a id="01-section-1"></a>

## Initial setup

### Choose your calendar systems

Tap the button to get started in the introduction shown after the first launch. On the calendar selection screen, turn on the switches for the calendar systems you want to use, then tap Save. Enable at least one calendar system. On the next screen, which sets the display order, touch and hold “=” at the right end of a row and drag it up or down to arrange the calendar systems, then tap Next.

### Choose whether to connect device calendars

The external calendar connection screen lets you set up access to events registered on your Android device. To connect them, follow the instructions on screen and grant both read and write permissions for calendars. You can also set this up later under <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/settings.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Gear"> (Settings) > event management. See [Syncing Device Calendars](07-calendar-sync.md) for details.

Choose the option to sync if you want to connect them, or the option to skip for now if you want to set this up later.

<a id="01-section-2"></a>

## Try the basics

### Switch calendar systems

Tap the calendar system name and “▼” at the top left of the main screen, then choose the calendar system to display. Enabled calendar systems appear in this list. To change the available choices, open <img class="button-icon" src="../../assets/icons/settings.svg" width="20" height="20" alt="Gear"> (Settings) > calendar display settings.

### View dates and events

In month view, tap a date to open its events and holidays. Tap an event to view its details. To move between months, swipe left or right, or tap the year and month at the top of the screen to use the year/month picker.

### Add an event

Tap “+” (add event) at the bottom right of the main screen, enter a title and date/time, then tap Save. If you select a date before adding an event, that date is filled in on the entry screen. See [Creating and Editing Events](04-events.md) for details.

## Using the app offline

Calendar displays and events stored on your device are available without a network connection. Adding or refreshing iCal subscriptions requires a connection. Device calendar synchronization depends on Android's calendar features and permissions. Depending on the sync status, it may take time for the latest events to appear.

| Action | Without a network connection |
| --- | --- |
| Switch calendar systems; add, edit, or delete in-app events | Available |
| Configure categories and themes | Available |
| View previously fetched connected or subscribed events | You can view the information stored on your device |
| Fetch a new public calendar or refresh one | A connection is required. Refreshing after adding a calendar also requires account integration to be enabled |
| Update events between device accounts and the cloud | Depends on the network connection and sync status of Android and the account |
