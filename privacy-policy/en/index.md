# WorldCalendar Privacy Policy

Originally established: September 13, 2026

Effective date of this revision: To be determined.

WorldCalendar (the “App”) is provided by an individual developer using the name “takuborn” on Google Play. This policy explains information handled, purposes, transmission to external services, retention, deletion and contact procedures.

## 1. Information Handled by the App

No App-specific account registration or login is required. Event titles, dates, times, notes, locations, reminders and settings are mainly stored on your device for event management, notifications and widgets. Terms acceptance time, version, language and app version are also stored locally.

Event contents are not sent to a server managed by the developer. However, the advertising SDK, calendar integration, ICS subscriptions and Android backup described below may process information outside the device.

## 2. Advertising with Google AdMob

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for AdMob banner and native ads. Google and other advertising providers may process advertising IDs (AAID), App Set IDs and other identifiers, IP addresses and approximate locations derived from them, ad views/clicks and other interactions, app/device information, and diagnostic/performance information. Purposes include serving ads, measuring performance, managing frequency and preventing fraud.

The App requests non-personalized ads (NPA). NPA does not select ads based on interests inferred from past behavior, but identifiers and other data may still be processed for frequency management, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

On supported Android devices, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in device settings; steps vary by OS and other conditions. This does not delete other identifiers or retained advertising data. The App has no separate control to refuse or withdraw from advertising-related processing.

See [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure?hl=en), [NPA explained](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7676680?hl=en), and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

## 3. External Calendar Integration

With your calendar permission and settings, the App uses Android Calendar Provider to read calendars/events and save, update or delete titles, descriptions, locations, dates, times, reminders and related information in your chosen destination.

If you select an external account such as Google Calendar, its synchronization settings can also reflect events in that service. Check that service's terms and privacy policy. See [Calendar Integration Help](../../helps/en/07-calendar-sync.md).

## 4. ICS Calendar Subscriptions

The App connects to your registered ICS feed URL to retrieve events. Your source IP address, URL/query, update-check headers and related information may reach the provider or redirect destination. Connection records may be retained under their policies.

Local event contents are not appended to retrieval requests. Information in the URL itself reaches the destination. Choose trusted providers and keep private subscription URLs confidential. See [Subscription Help](../../helps/en/08-subscriptions.md).

## 5. OS Backup and Device Transfer

The event database and some settings, including terms acceptance records, are eligible for Android backup and device transfer. Depending on OS, device, account and user settings, they may be stored by a backup service or restored to another device, rather than stored on the developer's server.

Backup/restoration is not guaranteed. Uninstalling may not delete backups. See [Backup Help](../../helps/en/10-backup-migration.md) and OS/service settings.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

### On-Device Data

Events and related data remain until you delete them. Use the App's deletion functions, clear its storage or uninstall to remove app-specific local data. This is separate from deletion by external services.

### AdMob

Advertising retention/deletion depends on Google's and other providers' purposes, contracts and policies. The developer cannot provide confirmed details of the retention periods and deletion request procedures for individual advertising data applicable to this App. Use Section 8 and [Google's information management/deletion guidance](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en). The developer cannot identify and delete all advertising data held by Google.

### External Calendars and ICS Providers

Delete external calendar events using that service and check after synchronization. Disconnecting or uninstalling does not delete them. Unsubscribing from ICS does not delete the provider's events or connection logs. Check each provider's retention/deletion policies.

### OS Backups

Retention, deletion and restoration depend on the OS/service. Manage backups in device/account settings. The developer cannot delete your backups directly.

### Inquiries

Email addresses, messages, attachments, correspondence and received consent records are used for responses, investigation, rights requests and checking expressed consent. Do not send unnecessary event details or sensitive information.

Records are normally retained for 1 year after resolution and deleted monthly from both Google Groups and receiving/forwarding mailboxes, normally within a maximum of 1 year and 1 month. Legally required retention or retention for ongoing disputes is limited to the necessary scope and duration.

## 7. External Providers and Handling Outside Japan

Google processes AdMob advertising data. The contracting entity for AdMob is Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd. The contracting entity does not necessarily identify every Google or other recipient/processor or processing country.

Inquiries use Google LLC's free consumer Google Groups and Gmail, under Google's Terms and Privacy Policy. External calendar, ICS and backup providers handle their respective information. Information may be processed outside Japan.

For inquiries, review the [notice and consent checkbox](/en/contact/) and agree before composing an email. The body includes your consent statement and notice version; the received message is used to check your expressed consent. No automatic sending occurs. Direct emails and messages missing consent are not automatically treated as consented.

Processing on the developer's instructions is distinguished from providers' own purposes. Information and consent procedures are provided as required for the processing concerned. Posting this policy or accepting the Terms does not replace any separately required consent.

## 8. Contact and User Rights

Questions and requests for access, correction, deletion, suspension of use or third-party provision and other rights are accepted at [worldcalendar-app@googlegroups.com (notice before sending)](/en/contact/). Information about personal data held by the developer that must be made available under applicable law is also provided without delay on request through this address.

Normally, receipt and next steps are explained within 7 days. This is not a deletion completion deadline; statutory deadlines take precedence.

Identity checks use the minimum necessary information. For inquiry-record deletion, normally reply from the original address and provide details such as the inquiry date; identity documents are not normally requested. If you cannot use that address, checks are explained individually. Deletion is assessed for each request without waiting for normal retention to expire.

If information cannot be identified or retention duties or other reasons prevent fulfillment, reasons and available procedures are explained. External-provider procedures may be necessary. Deleting local data or an advertising ID differs from deleting data held by Google.

Rights requests are not automatically refused solely for lack of consent to overseas handling. Minors should contact the developer with a parent/guardian where necessary, depending on their ability to understand the notice. A checkbox alone does not establish age, capacity or guardian consent.

On the developer's side, access to inquiry contents is restricted to the developer. Management Google accounts use two-step verification and management devices use screen locks. Questions about safeguards are also accepted above.

## 9. Changes and Revision History

This policy is revised as laws, features or information handling change. Changes and effective dates are announced here and on the official website, and in Google Play release notes with the next app update. Any required separate explanation/consent procedure is provided separately.
