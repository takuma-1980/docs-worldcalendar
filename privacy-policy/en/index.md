# WorldCalendar Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

WorldCalendar (the “App”) respects users' privacy and handles user information in accordance with the following policy.

## 1. Information Handled by the App

Events, reminders, and settings created in the App are generally stored on the user's device. The developer does not collect their contents.

Events saved to a device calendar are stored through Android's calendar features and may also be reflected in connected services such as Google, depending on account synchronization settings. Subscribing to an ICS feed connects to the provider registered by the user to retrieve events. Eligible data may also be stored and restored through Android backup and device transfer, depending on device settings and other conditions. See [Backup Help](../../helps/en/10-backup-migration.md) for details. These processes are also subject to the respective providers' terms and privacy policies.

## 2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect or share device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, app interaction information, diagnostic information, and other information for ad serving, usage analysis, and fraud prevention.

For details, please see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Data Storage and Deletion

Events, reminders, and settings stored on the device by the App can be deleted through the App's features or by uninstalling the App.
Data stored in connected calendars or OS backups may not be deleted merely by uninstalling the App. Check the respective service or device settings for deletion methods.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be revised in response to changes in laws or the App's features, among other reasons.
