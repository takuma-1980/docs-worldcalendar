CONTACT

Get in touch

Send bug reports, feature requests or other questions by email.

Contact by email

worldcalendar-app@googlegroups.com

Please do not send private event details, personal information, secret ICS URLs or passwords.

Check troubleshooting before contacting us →

What to include

  • Type of inquiry: bug report, feature request or other
  • App version, Android version and device model
  • Selected calendar and device time zone
  • Steps to reproduce, expected result and actual result
  • How often it happens, and when it occurred
  • Screenshots with personal information removed, if helpful
