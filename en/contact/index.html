CONTACT

Get in touch

Contact us by email about bugs, feature requests or information handling.

Revision draft for local review. This revised contact process has not been formally published.

Before sending an inquiry

The dedicated address is worldcalendar-app@googlegroups.com. Inquiries are received, delivered, stored and answered using the free consumer Google Groups and Gmail services provided by Google LLC (United States). Review this notice and the linked materials before proceeding to the consent checkbox.

Information and purposes

The sender name (if included in the email), email address, subject, message, attachments, information accompanying transmission and receipt, and replies and handling history are used to respond, investigate issues and handle rights requests. You do not have to provide your real name.

Do not send other people's personal information, sensitive information such as health details, private event details, identity documents, secret ICS URLs or passwords. Remove unnecessary information from screenshots too.

Handling outside Japan by Google

Inquiry information is disclosed to Google LLC as the provider of these services and may be handled outside Japan. Google LLC is located in the United States; this does not mean all processing and storage occur only there. The developer does not specify the individual server countries used by these free services. See Google's Privacy Policy for worldwide processing, affiliates and external processing providers.

US privacy rules and Google's safeguards

The US privacy system differs from Japan's. Materials published by Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission describe the absence of a comprehensive federal personal information protection law, alongside sector-specific laws such as those governing electronic communications and state-level rules. Review the Commission's US legal-system information (Japanese).

Google describes safeguards and access restrictions. Gmail describes encryption of messages and attachments in transit and at rest. This does not mean encryption that only the developer can decrypt, or that Google does not handle the information. Review Google's safeguards and Gmail's privacy protections and controls. This contact process does not operate under a business Google Workspace contract or its data processing terms.

Retention, deletion and your rights

On the developer's side, only the developer can view inquiry contents. Records are normally retained for one year after resolution and deleted monthly from both Google Groups and the mailbox, within a maximum of one year and one month after resolution. See Sections 6 and 8 of the Privacy Policy, including limited retention needed by law or for an ongoing dispute.

If you do not consent, do not send personal information through this email route. You can read the public help pages without consenting. Requests such as deletion are not automatically refused solely because you have not given blanket consent to overseas handling. Direct emails are not automatically treated as consent; the developer checks the expressed consent and basis for processing and minimizes additional disclosure. Confirmation after receipt is not applied retroactively as consent before receipt.

Check troubleshooting before contacting us

Minors and changes to the consent statement

Contact the developer with a parent or guardian where necessary, depending on your ability to understand this notice. A checkbox alone does not establish age, capacity or guardian consent. Edited or missing consent is assessed from the message actually received; missing text is not filled in or treated as consent.
